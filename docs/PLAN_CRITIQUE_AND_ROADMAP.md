# Critique of the blueprint and this build, and the phased plan

Written for: the project lead, the clinical reviewer and the district partner. Nothing here is approved
clinical content. Module sign-off stays in `clinical_signoff.json`.

## 1. Where the attached blueprint is right

- Replace a register, never add one. This is the e-Tracker lesson: adoption fell from 58.1% to 22.8% of
  Savannah facilities when staff had to keep paper and the tablet side by side. It should decide the
  roadmap, not sit as one principle among eight.
- Include the household decider, with her consent, before any call. Modules 2, 4 and 6 depend on it.
- Offline by default, AI suggests and the worker decides, danger signs follow fixed rules.
- Measure process steps, not "lives saved". A pilot in a few zones cannot show survival change.
- Build on the Community Health Toolkit rather than a new stack. The CHT is AGPL-3.0, and an
  implementation business is a normal model around it.

Gaps the blueprint does not name:

1. **No clinical lead, no sign-off.** Every clinical rule (newborn visit days, danger-sign thresholds,
   referral deadlines, dose tables) is unapproved. The PILOT banner is correct, but the pilot cannot
   start without a named clinical advisor.
2. **Automatic data-protection duties.** Recordings of caregivers are personal data. Registration with
   the Data Protection Commission and a retention and access policy must exist before any recording.
   The retention rules in `voice/recordings.js` are a developer proposal only.
3. **Hosting.** Free cloud does not meet the residency rule the blueprint sets. A real pilot needs a GHS
   or Ghana-hosted server; the free developer server is for made-up data only.
4. **Two sources of truth.** The second report describes `C:\Users\Owner\northern-health-platform`, a
   different copy on another laptop, with a different module state. This repository has uncommitted
   changes on top of `2fcdd8e`. Both point at the same GitHub remote.

## 3. Where this build is wrong or weak

1. **Voice module number.** The voice form was filed under module 2. The blueprint says module 4. Fixed
   in `clinical_signoff.json` and the brief; modules 4 and 5 were added as unapproved.
2. **Uncommitted work.** Many changes are not in git. Nothing has been committed or pushed in this
   session, because commits need your instruction. Reconcile with the other copy before committing.
3. **Speech recognition is not usable yet.** The Akan model measured 42.7% word error rate on 20 WAXAL
   clips (747 words), with one clip at 2.71. A keyword matcher on that output will sometimes pick the
   wrong reply. The safe design is the CHW choosing the topic from a menu, with server speech recognition
   kept off until it is validated on real caregiver speech.
4. **The keyword matcher is untested on real speech.** The 14 caregiver phrases were written by the
   developer. They prove the matcher does what it was told, not that it works for caregivers.
5. **Danger-sign content is duplicated.** The CHT reference form, our maternal danger assessment and the
   voice replies can disagree. The blueprint flags this; the clinical advisor must choose one source.
6. **Akan dataset reuse needs a licence check.** CC BY 4.0 allows commercial reuse. The source phrases are
   LLM-generated; each phrase needs clinical review before it becomes a caregiver message.

## 4. The plan: phases, gates and owners

Phases follow the blueprint's roadmap, but each has a gate. A module that misses its gate is fixed or
dropped before the next starts.

**Phase 0: agreements (blocks everything; people, not code).** Owner: project lead.
- Register a not-for-profit. Needed for DPC registration, grants and district agreements.
- Name a clinical advisor (midwife or public-health doctor) and sign them off per module.
- District partner: written agreement on which paper registers each module retires. Gate A.
- Research partner for ethics review (for example, Navrongo Health Research Centre).
- Ask GHS for the current national protocols and DHIMS-2 data-element IDs.
- Ask Khaya in writing for pricing, language coverage and terms for generated audio in a health app.

**Phase 1: Modules 1, 6, 7 and the danger-sign core.** Owner: developer, then clinical advisor.
- Built and tested in CHT forms and tasks. Remaining: field time-budget tests (blueprint targets) with
  five nurses; clinical sign-off; a hazard log per release.
- Gate: sign-off recorded; field test passes the time budgets; no dangerous error.

**Phase 2: Module 2 (referral loop) and Module 3 (nutrition).** Owner: developer, nutritionist.
- Referral loop needs a server for the SMS-code confirmation. Build the logic now; run it on a test server.
- Nutrition: plans are drafts. The nutritionist decides each one with `record_decision.py`.
- Gate: nutritionist approval; price data no older than three months (the WFP series ends in July 2023).

**Phase 3: Module 4 (voice) and Module 5 (CHPS workflows and DHIMS-2).** Owner: developer, GHS.
- Module 5 is the adoption gate. Needs GHS registers and DHIMS-2 IDs. The demo push works but is test
  data only. The DHIMS-2 import file the district already accepts comes first; automatic push second.
- Module 4: menu-first offline voice. Content comes from reviewed phrases (Akan dataset, after clinical
  review) and Khaya audio only after its terms are confirmed in writing. Speech recognition stays off
  until it passes a caregiver test set.
- eCHIS: blocked on access. The route is in `ECHIS_ACCESS_GUIDE.md`.
- Gate: content signed off by a native speaker and a health worker; district accepts the report file.

**Later: scheduled calls, risk model, Ga/Ewe/Dagbani content.** Needs a grant, 12 months of local data,
and content that does not exist yet. Not started, and not to be started without a funded plan.
