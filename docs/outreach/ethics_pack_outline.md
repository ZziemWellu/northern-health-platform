# Ethics review pack: outline

Route: the Ghana Health Service Ethics Review Committee (GHS-ERC), through a research partner or
directly. Submission is two weeks before the next committee meeting. Confirm the current checklist with
the committee's secretariat.

**Documents the committee asks for** (from its published procedures):
1. Principal investigator's application form.
2. Cover letter and institutional support letter from the host institution.
3. Confirmation letters from any collaborating institution (the Regional Health Directorate, the research partner).
4. A statement that the researchers will comply with ethical principles.
5. The GHS-ERC administrative information form and checklist.
6. The full protocol with an executive summary: objectives, study design, setting, population, sampling,
   inclusion and exclusion criteria, data collection, data management and storage, and analysis.
7. Consent materials: the verbal consent script in the local language, and the information sheet.
8. Risk and benefit assessment, including harm from calls to family members and from a wrong danger-sign
   decision, and the referral arrangements when a danger sign is found.
9. Data protection arrangements: Act 843 registration, hosting location, retention and deletion, access log.
10. The clinical advisor's review record (once the advisor has reviewed the content).

**Our protocol, in brief:** a pilot evaluation of process measures (records made, referrals completed,
newborn visits within the protocol window) in a stepped rollout across zones. Outcome measures are not
claimed without outcome data.

**Not yet decided:** the research partner, and whether this is an evaluation (which can run under the
district agreement) or research (which needs the full pack).
