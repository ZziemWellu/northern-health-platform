# Not-for-profit registration: checklist (company limited by guarantee)

Route: the Registrar-General's Department, Office of the Registrar of Companies (ORC). Confirm current
forms, fees and portals at orc.gov.gh before filing. The steps below come from published guidance and
are not a substitute for the ORC's own instructions.

1. **Choose the name** and check it is free at the Registrar-General's Department (name search).
   Reserve it if the search allows.
2. **Get a TIN** for every executive council member, subscriber and the secretary. Anyone without one
   completes a TIN application.
3. **Prepare the forms:** Form 3B (company details, executive council, digital address, registered office),
   Forms 26(A) and 26(B) (consent of directors and secretary), the application letter and the NGO profile form.
4. **Prepare the constitution** (memorandum and articles) with the objects stated clearly, for example
   "to develop and provide open-source digital health tools for community health in Northern Ghana".
5. **Collect the identity documents:** notarised passport biodata pages for members, subscribers and secretary.
6. **Pay the fee** (published as about GH¢490–520; confirm the current amount with the ORC).
7. **Submit** the forms, constitution, name reservation and documents to the ORC.
8. **Record the certificate of incorporation** and the registration number. Every later document uses that number.

**Who signs.** The directors and subscribers, personally. Nobody else can sign for the organisation at this stage.

**After registration.** Open a bank account in the organisation's name; register for tax as the law requires;
then file the DPC registration (see the DPC checklist).
