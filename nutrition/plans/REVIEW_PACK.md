# Feeding plans for the nutritionist's review

Written for: the nutritionist or dietitian reviewing the plans, and the Regional Director of Health.

Nothing in this pack is approved. Each plan is a draft until the reviewer records a decision with `record_decision.py`. The CHT app shows only approved plans.

Plans in this pack: 18. Status now: 0 approved.

## Questions for the reviewer

1. Is the energy target right for this age group (200, 300 or 550 kcal from complementary food)?
2. Are the portions (grams per day) realistic for a child at this age, and for a family to prepare?
3. Is the list of excluded foods right, and do the reasons still hold? (Prices were not consistent or data was unreliable.)
4. Is the referral and supplementation wording correct under the current GHS protocol? Which product and route should the CHW name?
5. Is the price region and season split right for this plan (for example, Savannah and North East use Northern prices)?

## Decision guide

- **Approve as drafted**: the plan is right for families in this region, season and age group.
- **Approve with changes**: write the change. The plan stays a draft until the changed plan is rebuilt and approved again.
- **Reject**: the plan must not reach families. The app will keep giving standard GHS counselling for it.
