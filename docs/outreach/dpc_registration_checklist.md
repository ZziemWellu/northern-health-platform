# Data Protection Commission registration: checklist

Route: the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.org.gh). A data controller who processes personal
data must register under Act 843, section 27. The registration is valid for two years and is renewed.
Confirm the current form, fee and portal on the Commission's website before filing.

**Prerequisite:** the organisation's certificate of incorporation (see the NGO checklist).

**Information the form asks for** (from the Commission's published requirements):
1. Legal name, registration number and entity type.
2. Address and sector.
3. Description of the services and of the processing.
4. Categories of personal data (for the pilot: visit records, household details, referral records, and
   caregiver contact details only where a consented message is sent).
5. Special category data: health data is sensitive, so state it and the reason.
6. Purposes of processing.
7. Approximate number of data subjects and records per year.
8. Countries outside Ghana where any data is processed. State "none" if the hosting is in Ghana. Free
   development hosting outside Ghana uses only made-up data, and must be stated as such.
9. Security measures: encryption, access control, logging, backup location, and staff training.
10. Annual turnover in Ghana cedis, which may be nil for a new organisation.
11. Group company status.

**Then:** pay the fee, file the form, receive the certificate, and display or keep it available for inspection.

**Ongoing:** renew before the two years end. Report any breach to the Commission as the Act requires.

**Note on the pending law.** The Data Protection Bill 2025 would replace Act 843 and tighten localisation for
health records. Register under Act 843 now and check the Bill's status before the renewal.
