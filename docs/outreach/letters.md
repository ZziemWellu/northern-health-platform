# Letters: drafts to adapt and send

Written for: the project lead. Each draft is short on purpose: its job is to get a meeting, not to
explain everything. Attach the one-page summary (`docs/pitch/ONE_PAGE_SUMMARY.md`, as a PDF) and
offer the demo link or the two-minute video. Fill the [brackets]; remove what does not apply.
Nothing here is sent until you send it.

Order: 1 and 2 first (they unlock everything else), then 3 and 4, then 5 once the pilot has results.

---

## 1. Kumbungu District Health Directorate: request for a meeting

**To:** The District Director of Health Services, Kumbungu
**Subject:** Request for a meeting: a free digital tool for CHPS registers and the monthly report

Dear [Director's name],

I am [name], [role, e.g. a PhD researcher at [university]]. I have built an open-source app for CHPS
nurses that does three things: it ranks each nurse's visits by urgency, applies danger-sign rules at
the visit and raises a referral, and produces the monthly counts in DHIMS-2's import format, so the
hand tally from the paper registers can stop.

It is ready for a small test. Before anything is used with patients it needs a clinical review and
your Directorate's agreement, and I would like to ask for your advice on both.

May I have 30 minutes with you, the District Public Health Nurse and the HMIS officer to show it on a
phone? I would particularly value your view on:
1. which paper registers it should replace first;
2. whether a test with five nurses for six weeks would be useful to the district;
3. the language list for Kumbungu's communities.

The app is free and open source, works offline, and keeps data in Ghana. Nothing is used with real
patients without your written agreement, ethics approval and a clinical sign-off.

Yours faithfully,
[Name, phone, email]
Attached: one-page summary.
