4. Module 4: caregiver voice replies (menu-first)

Read from voice/replies.json. The CHW picks the topic on the phone; the approved reply appears offline. No speech recognition is used.

emergency

This may be an emergency. Go to the nearest health facility now. If you cannot travel, call your CHW or the emergency number right away.

Twi (first draft, not checked by a Twi speaker): Eyi betumi ayɛ ahohia. Kɔ ayaresabea a ɛbɛn wo ho nnɛ. Sɛ wontumi nantew a, frɛ wo CHW anaa ahohia nɔma nnɛ.

PILOT wording, not approved

visit_date

Your next clinic visit is on the date your CHW gave you. Please come. If you do not know the date, ask your CHW.

Twi (first draft, not checked by a Twi speaker): Wo clinic nsɔhwɛ a edi hɔ no yɛ da a wo CHW kyerɛɛ wo. Sɛ wonnim da no a, bisa wo CHW.

PILOT wording, not approved

transport

If transport is a problem, tell your CHW. Your CHW can help you plan a ride to the facility.

Twi (first draft, not checked by a Twi speaker): Sɛ akwantu yɛ ɔhaw a, ka kyerɛ wo CHW. Wo CHW betumi aboa wo ma wonhyehyɛ akwantu.

PILOT wording, not approved

feeding

For feeding questions, your CHW will help. Ask them to show you an approved feeding plan for your baby.

PILOT wording, not approved

Sorry, I did not understand. Please call your CHW, or ask a family member to call them for you.

Twi (first draft, not checked by a Twi speaker): Me ho ayɛ wo, minnte nea woka no. Frɛ wo CHW, anaa ka sɛ obi nfrɛ wɔn ma wo.

PILOT wording, not approved

5. What is needed before any of this can be used

  • A named clinical advisor signs each module (section 1).
  • A nutritionist approves or rejects each plan (section 3).
  • A Twi speaker and a health worker check each voice reply (section 4).
  • The Ghana Health Service protocol governs the postnatal schedule (National Safe Motherhood Protocol: at least three visits, within 48 hours, day 6 or 7, and 6 weeks).
  • A district agrees in writing to the paper registers it retires. Ethics review and Data Protection Commission registration come before any real data.
