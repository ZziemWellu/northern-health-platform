# Memorandum of understanding: pilot of a community health records system (draft)

**Status: DRAFT for discussion. Not agreed. Not signed.**

**Parties.** [Organisation name and registration number, once issued] ("the Organisation") and [the
District Health Directorate of [District], Northern Region] ("the Directorate").

**1. Purpose.** A six-month pilot in [number] CHPS zones of [District], to test whether an offline
community health system can record visits, track referrals and produce the monthly district report with
less double entry than the current paper registers.

**2. What the pilot covers.** Only the modules named in Schedule 1 (initially: newborn and postnatal
visits, referral tracking, and a monthly reporting export file). Nutrition plans and voice support are
not part of the pilot unless the Directorate agrees in writing after their clinical review.

**3. Paper registers.** The Directorate names, in Schedule 2, each paper register the platform will
replace. The pilot does not run in parallel with a register that is not named there. Where a register is
named, its entries are recorded in the platform and not in the paper book from the start date.

**4. Clinical content.** The Organisation will use only clinical content that a named clinical advisor has
approved, recorded in writing. Until then the platform shows a visible notice that content is not approved.
The Directorate's clinical lead may request changes at any time, and the Organisation will act on them.

**5. Data.** Data are stored in Ghana. Only the data in Schedule 3 are collected. Caregivers give consent
before any recording or call. Access is restricted to named people. The Organisation will provide the
Directorate with a copy of all data on request and delete it at the end of the pilot unless the
Directorate asks for it to be transferred. The Organisation complies with the Data Protection Act, 2012
(Act 843), and with any later law that replaces it.

**6. Messages.** No live SMS or voice messages are sent to any person until the Directorate and the
Organisation agree in writing on the message texts, the sender name and the cost.

**7. Training and support.** The Organisation trains the nurses and records who was trained. The
Directorate names a focal person for the pilot.

**8. Evaluation.** Process measures are reported monthly (Schedule 4). Outcome claims are made only with
outcome data. Results are shared with the Directorate before any publication.

**9. Costs.** Each party bears its own costs unless Schedule 5 says otherwise.

**10. Ending the pilot.** Either party may end the pilot with 30 days' written notice. The Organisation
returns or deletes the data as in section 5.
