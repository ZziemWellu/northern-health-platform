# Pilot outreach and registration pack

Prepared for the project lead. Nothing here is signed, sent or filed. Each document has blanks where a
named person, an address or a decision is needed. Nobody can sign for a clinical advisor, a district
or a regulator.

## Order of work

0. Settle the institutional home (`institutional_home.md`) and send letters 1 and 2 (`letters.md`):
   the district and a host institution unlock everything below.
1. File the not-for-profit. The DPC registration, grants and district agreement all need it.
2. Send the three quote requests now. Hosting decisions take time, and quotes are the cheapest evidence
   of seriousness an NGO or investor will ask for.
3. Recruit the clinical advisor. Nothing clinical can be shown to a district without one.
4. Once the organisation exists, file DPC registration and open the district conversation with the draft MoU.
5. Submit the ethics pack through a research partner, or through the GHS Ethics Review Committee.

## Two rules for every document

- Nothing is described as approved, registered, signed or agreed until it is.
- Every claim about a data centre, a fee or a process is marked as needing confirmation until a written answer is in hand.
